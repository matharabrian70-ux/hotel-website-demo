Welcome to Amani House

Some places ask you to do less.

Hidden between the textures of the earth and the rhythm of the wild, Amani House is imagined as a sanctuary for travellers who value meaningful moments over hurried itineraries.

Wake to birdsong, take the long way to breakfast, and let the day unfold at its own pace. Every space is designed to feel considered, calm and connected to its surroundings.
